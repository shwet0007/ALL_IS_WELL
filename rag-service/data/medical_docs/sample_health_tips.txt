TITLE: PREGNANCY NUTRITION GUIDE

During pregnancy, your body needs extra nutrients to support your baby's growth.

KEY NUTRIENTS:
1. Folic Acid: Helps prevent birth defects. Found in green leafy vegetables, citrus fruits, and beans.
2. Iron: Important for your baby's blood supply. Eat red meat, chicken, fish, and fortified cereals.
3. Calcium: Builds strong bones and teeth for your baby. Good sources include milk, cheese, yogurt, and tofu.
4. Protein: Essential for growth. Include lean meat, poultry, fish, eggs, and beans in your diet.

FOODS TO AVOID:
- Raw or undercooked seafood, eggs, and meat.
- Unpasteurized milk and cheese.
- Certain fish high in mercury (e.g., shark, swordfish).
- Excess caffeine (limit to 200mg per day).

HYDRATION:
Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Water helps form the placenta and the amniotic sac.

WEIGHT GAIN:
Healthy weight gain varies, but typically ranges from 25-35 pounds for women of normal weight. Consult your doctor for personalized advice.

EXERCISE:
Stay active! Walking, swimming, and prenatal yoga are generally safe. Avoid high-impact sports. Always listen to your body.

DISCLAIMER: This information is for awareness only and does not substitute professional medical advice. Always consult your doctor.
